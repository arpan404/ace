Worked for 2m 14s3:54 PM

The flake comes from a fixed timer in the payment poller, not from the test itself.

  • pollPayment() retries on a hard-coded 50 ms interval; the test asserts after 40 ms.
  • Under load the first poll lands late, so the assertion sees the previous status.

I made the poller await the first tick and gave the test a waitFor with a 500 ms ceiling. 612 tests passed locally, 20 runs in a row.
