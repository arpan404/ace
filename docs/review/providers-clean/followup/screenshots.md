# Provider and account polish review

Captured with the fake daemon on this worktree, using reduced motion. No live account home or real provider prompt was used.

Widths: 1440 and 390. Themes: light, dark, midnight, graphite, paper, slate and contrast. Every provider/account/Usage surface was reviewed alongside the mockup. The initial picker/composer checks cover light and dark; the chosen badges cover all seven themes.

930 local PNGs: 512 original captures, 364 comparisons with the mockup, 38 review sheets and 16 before/after composites. Full-resolution originals and comparisons stay at `/tmp/ace-orch/shots/ui-providers-clean/followup/`. The review sheets, before/after composites and selected full-resolution samples are attached below.

The earlier delivery is the Before image. After images show real provider marks, account badges and full names, aligned meter/action columns, secondary home paths and stacked mobile readings. Rename and badge editing use one keyboard-accessible dialog, retain failed drafts and offer Retry. Loading, empty and failure screens keep a clear next action.
