# Code Of Conduct

ace is maintained around direct, technical collaboration. Keep discussion focused on the work and make it possible for maintainers and contributors to reason clearly about issues, tradeoffs, and fixes.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Assume good faith while asking for evidence when claims are unclear.
- Keep issues and PRs focused on the technical problem.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope, timing, and review requirements.
- Redact secrets and private data before sharing logs or screenshots.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Repeated off-topic arguing after maintainers have made a scope decision.
- Posting secrets, private data, or exploit details publicly.
- Spam, low-effort automation, or intentionally disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or delete comments; close issues or PRs; block accounts; or report abuse to GitHub when needed.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub.
