# Weekly review

Use this at the end of a week, or when the person asks where things stand.

## Gather

Look through what the person has connected and what you already know from this week:
calendar, notes, tasks, messages you handled, and your own earlier notes. Don't ask the person
for what you can find.

## Write

1. **Done this week.** What finished, one line each, grouped by project. Leave out routine work
   unless the person tracks it.
2. **Still open.** What was started or promised and isn't finished, with how long it has been
   open. Put anything older than two weeks first.
3. **Waiting on others.** Who owes what, and since when.
4. **Next week.** Fixed commitments from the calendar, then the three things that matter most,
   as the person has said or as the open items suggest. Mark which are your suggestion.
5. **One question.** If something looks stuck or contradictory, ask about it in one sentence.

## Keep it short

The review should be read in two minutes. Link to details rather than copying them. Keep a note
of what you suggested, so next week's review can say what happened to it.
