# Inbox triage

Use this to go through what arrived since the last check, in mail or chat.

## Sort

Put each message or thread in one group:

- **Needs you today:** a direct question to the person, a deadline within two days, someone
  blocked on them, anything from people they've said matter.
- **Can wait:** worth reading or answering, but not today.
- **Noise:** newsletters, notifications, receipts and automated mail, unless the person asked
  to see a kind of them.

When unsure between two groups, choose the more urgent one and say why in a few words.

## Report

List "Needs you today" first, most urgent at the top. For each: who, what they want, and by
when, in one line. Then a count of "Can wait" with the senders, and a count of noise.

## Draft replies

For messages that need an answer, draft a short reply in the person's usual tone: a few
sentences, the answer first. Mark anything you aren't sure of. Never send, archive, delete or
mark anything read without the person's word.

## Remember

Keep a short note of senders and threads the person treats as important or as noise, and use
it next time.
