# Problems and editing a Suit

## Editing an existing Suit

1. Read the folder whole: `suit.json`, `suit.css`, every ornament and scene. Find what each part of the request maps to (a field, a rule, a file) before changing anything.
2. Keep the Suit's identity: its id, name, ramp hue and accent stay unless the person asked to change them. A request like "warmer" moves the ramp's hue a few degrees in every surface and text level together, in both schemes; it never edits one field alone.
3. Change the smallest set of fields and rules that does what was asked, save, and run the loop from **Preview**. A Suit that is on (picked or previewed) updates in the open app on every save, so saved half-edits show: write a change whole, then save.
4. A Suit Settings lists under "Needs fixes before it can be used" has stored problems: run `suit_apply { mode: "preview", id }` to get them back as a list, then fix each one below.
5. To make a variant, copy the folder to a new id instead of editing the original.

## Reading a problem

Each problem is `{ scheme, check, field, line?, message }`. `scheme` is `light`, `dark` or `null` (both); `field` is the palette field, file or part the problem is in; `line` is the 1-based line in `suit.css` or an ornament. Fix that field in that scheme and nothing else, then preview again: the message names the measured value and the target, so you can see whether the fix moved far enough.

## Static checks

These run before anything applies; any one of them refuses the preview.

| Check         | Fails when                                                                                                                                                                                  | Fix                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `schema`      | `suit.json` isn't JSON, a value is outside its range, `id` differs from the folder, or neither scheme is set.                                                                               | Read `field` and the message; the ranges are in `suit-json.md`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `contrast`    | `text`, `muted`, `subtle` or `accentText` under 4.5:1 on a surface; `faint` or `accent` under 3:1 on `canvas`; `accentFg` under 4.5:1 on the accent; the terminal's foreground under 4.5:1. | Move the named text colour away from the surfaces, or the surfaces closer together. `accentText`: a step toward `text`. `accentFg`: white or near-black. `field: null` names a colour ace owns (a status word): change the surfaces under it.                                                                                                     |
| `accent-fit`  | `accent` is too close to a status colour.                                                                                                                                                   | Turn the hue 20–40° toward the middle of a gap (copper, bronze, warm orange, ice, rose-gold), or drop the chroma toward chalk or graphite.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `glass-floor` | Text can't read on glass over busy backdrops.                                                                                                                                               | Set that scheme's `glass` to `null`, or move `raised` and `text` further apart.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `css`         | A rule, selector or declaration in `suit.css` (or a `regions` value) is refused; `line` points at it.                                                                                       | The message says why: a class, id or element in a selector; an unknown part; a custom property not starting `--suit-`; `!important`; a `url()` outside `assets/`; a banned function; a non-frame property on a guarded part; an at-rule other than `@media`, `@keyframes` or `@font-face`; a nested rule. Rewrite that rule within `suit-css.md`. |
| `asset`       | A font in `fonts` is missing or isn't `.woff2`, `.woff`, `.ttf` or `.otf`.                                                                                                                  | Add the file under `assets/` or fix the path.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `ornament`    | An ornament file is missing, over 64 KB, or held something the allowlist drops (script, handler, link out).                                                                                 | Remove what the message names; keep plain shapes and `currentColor`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `scene`       | A file named in `scenes` is missing, or `scene.js` is empty.                                                                                                                                | Add the file or remove the entry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

## Live checks

These run on the person's open ace windows after a preview or keep, with `field` naming the part.

| Check           | Fails when                                                                                                            | Fix                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `live-contrast` | A part's text is under 4.5:1 (3:1 at 24 px, or 18.66 px bold) or its icons under 3:1 over what is actually behind it. | The message gives the colours as rendered. Make that part's background in suit.css more opaque or move it in lightness, lower the region's `reveal` over a busy scene, or dim the scene. |
| `protected`     | A guarded part on screen is hidden, faded, under its minimum size, or something covers its centre.                    | Remove the rule, ornament or `::before`/`::after` that hides, fades or overlaps it.                                                                                                      |
| `overflow`      | A part reaches past its region or spills its content, at the window's width or at 1024 and 760 px (simulated).        | Drop a fixed width, reduce padding or letter-spacing, or let the text wrap in that part.                                                                                                 |

## The rest of the result

- `live.source: "static"`: no window answered within the wait, so only the static checks ran. Open ace in ace's browser and take screenshots yourself.
- `next`: one sentence on what to do now; follow it.
- `live.devices`: which windows checked, in which scheme and at which widths.
- `live.unchecked`: parts over an `assets/` image or an image-drawing scene; judge their contrast from the screenshot.
- `live.notes`: why a device sent no screenshot (a browser tab can't capture itself).
- `status`: `previewing`, `kept`, `reverted`, `refused` (static problems; nothing applied), `not-found` (no folder with that id), `nothing-to-do` (no preview to keep or revert).
